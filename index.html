Questions

Does the price change in a traffic jam?

No. Prices are fixed and confirmed in advance, whatever the traffic or detours.

Do you provide child and baby seats?

Yes, baby seats and boosters are free. Ask for them when you book.

How do I meet Abdel at the airport?

Abdel waits at the exit after baggage claim holding a sign with your name.

How is my booking confirmed?

Your request goes to Abdel on WhatsApp. He checks the date and time and replies to confirm.

How can I pay?

Cash in euros, card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) on a mobile terminal in the car, or an online payment link on request.
